Rethink how your system manages capital equipment

Cohealo combines software, logistics, and customer success services to build a network for sharing equipment between your facilities, owned or affiliated.

Track Daily Utilization

Utilizing a lightweight feed from your IT system, Cohealo tracks equipment utilization across every facility, giving you a single view of what you own, where it is, and how often it's used.

Reposition assets in real time

On-demand, specially trained logistics ensure safe, timely mobilization of shared equipment. Automated scheduling and conflict management remove burden from clinical staff.

Inform Capital Planning

Cohealo customer success leaders leverage platform analytics to guide purchase reductions, eliminate rentals, and disrupt the replacement cycle, delivering a 3-5× return on your capital budget.
