Data-driven capital planning

Cohealo weighs each capital request against current equipment utilization and the option to source existing equipment from sister facilities.

Purchase equipment based on usage

Capital equipment is one of a health system's fastest-growing expenses. Cohealo brings transparency to capital planning with objective utilization data, so executives can prioritize what to buy and when.

Not just data dashboards. Real human analysis.

Every health system is different. Our customer success team works directly with OR directors and finance staff to meet your capital and operating expense reduction goals, combining the technology with real human judgment.

Identify duplicate requests, then share

Instead of buying one unit per facility, Cohealo identifies equipment that can be purchased once for multiple providers across the system. Finance moves further down its list, and providers get access to a greater variety of equipment.

Capital planning is where tracking and sharing pay off.

Tracking shows which assets sit idle. Sharing puts that capacity within reach of the facility that needs it. Capital planning applies that evidence to each purchase request before the check gets written, avoiding unnecessary purchases and shrinking the capital fleet.

A repeatable path from request to purchase

Each capital request moves through the same four checkpoints before a dollar gets committed.

Step 1

Surface idle capacity

Tracking data flags equipment that sits underused across the network, so every review starts with what is already on hand.

Step 2

Run an opportunity assessment

Each request is weighed against that idle capacity to see whether it can be met without a purchase.

Step 3

Map sharing across facilities

When a purchase is still the right call, Cohealo lines up the other sites that can use the same unit, so it is shared from day one.

Step 4

Score and prioritize

Requests are ranked by projected savings and financial impact, so finance and value analysis know what to approve first.
