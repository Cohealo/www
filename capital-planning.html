Solutions · Capital Planning

Data-driven capital planning

Cohealo evaluates your capital requests, weighing each potential purchase in the context of current equipment utilization and the potential to source existing equipment from sister facilities.

Purchase equipment based on usage

Health systems are under more pressure than ever before to find savings, and capital equipment is one of the fastest growing expenses. Cohealo improves the transparency around capital planning by providing objective data on current equipment utilization, so executives can prioritize what to buy and when to buy it.

Not just data dashboards. Real human analysis.

Every health system is different. That's why Cohealo's customer success team works directly with OR directors and finance staff to meet the system's unique capital and operational expense reduction goals, combining our technology with real human judgment.

Identify duplicate requests, and then share

During the capital planning process, instead of buying a single resource for each facility, Cohealo identifies equipment that can be purchased for multiple providers across the health system, so finance moves further down its list of requests, and providers get access to a greater variety of equipment.

Capital planning isn't a standalone initiative. It's a critical source of insight for avoiding unnecessary purchases and reducing the size of your capital fleet.

Every purchase decision Cohealo helps a health system make is powered by the same utilization data collected through everyday equipment tracking, and the same network visibility built through equipment sharing. Tracking shows which assets are sitting idle; sharing puts that idle capacity within reach of the facility that needs it. Capital planning is simply the moment that evidence gets applied to a purchase request, before the check gets written, not after.

How it works

A repeatable path from request to purchase

Each capital request moves through the same four checkpoints before a dollar gets committed.

Step 1

Surface idle capacity

Tracking data across the network flags equipment that's sitting underused, giving every capital review a starting point grounded in what's already on hand.

Step 2

Run an opportunity assessment

Each request gets weighed against that idle capacity to see whether it can be met without a purchase at all.

Step 3

Map sharing across facilities

When a purchase is still the right call, Cohealo lines up other sites that could use the same unit, so it's shared from day one instead of sitting idle at a single facility.

Step 4

Score and prioritize

Requests are ranked by projected savings and broader financial impact, so finance and value analysis know which purchases to green-light first.
