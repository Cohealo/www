Our company

We believe healthcare works better when hospitals work together, as a system

Cohealo is a technology and services company on a mission to make health systems financially stronger, by transforming how they track, share, and purchase capital equipment.

Our mission

Making healthcare more affordable by drastically reducing the amount of capital equipment health systems need to buy and maintain.

A hospital needs to be economically healthy so it can invest in the best clinicians, technology, and patient experiences. Cohealo was founded on the belief that collaboration among hospitals, powered by technology, could dramatically improve financial outcomes.

We bring transparency and collaboration into equipment management: one of the largest and most overlooked expenses for any health system. When hospitals share, everyone wins, patients included.

Enabling healthcare providers to deliver the highest quality care, at the most affordable price, by bringing hospitals together around the equipment they already own.

- Cohealo mission statement

What we stand for

Our values

These aren't words on a wall. They're the principles that guide every decision we make, from how we build our product to how we show up for our customers.

Trust and be trustworthy

We form strong relationships across our team and with our customers. We act with the utmost integrity and honesty, especially when it's hard.

Test, learn, iterate and improve

We're building something new. That means stepping outside our comfort zones, learning new skills, and pivoting as we unlock insights.

Act with empathy

We remember that we all have humanity in common. We create an open space to share and receive feedback; every voice contributes to our success.

Relentlessly pursue the problem

We doggedly investigate challenges, ask questions, listen to answers, and identify solutions, persistent and patient across a complex, high-stakes industry.

Customers as partners

We place customer needs at the center of everything. From frontline nurses and surgeons to finance and supply chain leaders; everyone should feel valued.

No robots here

We embrace the whole person at Cohealo. We focus on time well spent, not time on the clock, and we celebrate hard when we succeed.
